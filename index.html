How I work

  1. Understand

    I start with the decision someone needs to make, then trace the data behind it. Requirements and stakeholder alignment come before any code.

  2. Build

    I turn manual steps into automated pipelines, with SQL, Python, and BigQuery underneath and a dashboard or tool on top.

  3. Validate

    Every number gets checked against its source and reviewed with the people who know it best before anything goes live.

  4. Adopt

    I train the people who use it, gather feedback, and keep improving it, so it becomes the way the team works.
